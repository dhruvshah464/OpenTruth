Six kinds of proof.

Requirement and declared environment → Verification IR or expander → plan.json → independent runner → append-only A / O / E records → verdict.json → seal manifest.json. The planner can change; the proof machinery does not.

Discipline

The freeze is the product.

We do not schedule automatic requirement discovery, unfamiliar-repo analysis, multi-agent verification, production observability, verification marketplaces, enterprise dashboards, hosted URL scanning, or benchmark apps as the next engine step. The protocol evolves without redesigning runners, seal, or E-only verdict.json.
